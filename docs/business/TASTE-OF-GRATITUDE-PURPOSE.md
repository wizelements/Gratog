- Reserve products

These customers already know the products.

They may simply want to say:

- Do you have Strawberry Bliss?
- Can I get my regular gel?
- Can I pick this up?
- Can you deliver this?
- What do you have available today?
- Can I reserve two bottles?

Do not force returning customers through unnecessary education or market selection.

4.4 Availability-request customers

These customers want a product that may not be listed or confirmed.

4.5 Delivery customers

These customers need to know:

- Whether their area is supported
- The minimum order
- The delivery fee
- The expected delivery window
- Whether delivery requires manual confirmation

They must know the cost before payment.
